# Permanent redaction v2 acceptance

The adapter marks visible regions by dragging or by keyboard percentage controls, supports removal/page jumps and bounded undo/redo, and preserves its committed draft through Back. An explicit acknowledgement explains that every page becomes an image-only PDF and loses original selectable text, interactive forms, attachments, digital-signature validation, accessibility tags and metadata, including unmarked pages. Unmarked visible content, including form-value or signature appearances, remains readable as pixels.

The existing `redactPdf` engine builds a fresh PDF from rendered pages. It copies no original PDF objects. The result page renders the exact downloadable bytes; review never triggers a separate regeneration. Its preview is abortable and disposed when leaving results. Source limits are 40 MB and 15 pages, 120 areas and 50 history steps. The 40 MB cap is checked before reading bytes. Raster resolution has a 3.2 MP / 2,200-pixel per-page cap; higher requested DPI may hit this cap.

Seven browser journeys verify explicit consent, actual pointer and keyboard regions, undo/redo and page association, cancellation, recovery after the 15-page limit, 40 MB rejection before reading, cropped/rotated geometry, short landscape pages, and removal of a cancelled drag's temporary mask. Downloaded output is reopened with pdf-lib and PDF.js: two image-only pages, no searchable text, no original title/author, no attachments, no original form fields, and expected black/white pixels. The source fixture includes a real attachment and form field. A cropped/rotated source exports at its rendered dimensions; short-page mask bounds match the canvas and actual exported black pixels.

Fresh review found two important defects, both observed failing and then fixed: the overlay previously followed the paper's minimum height rather than the canvas; `pointercancel` previously left a visible temporary mask that was absent from the export. Masks now use exact canvas size/offset, and cancellation redraws only committed areas. The earlier single-pointer draft cannot be overwritten by a second pointer. Area controls are inert while a new page is rendering; export refuses an unfinished drag.

Verification: 59 v2 Chromium journeys and 200 units pass, as do typecheck/lint/build. Fresh three-browser CI on the published head is still required. This is destructive raster redaction with bounded image quality, not text-preserving semantic redaction, universal fidelity or legal certification. Broader real-document, physical-device, accessibility and memory acceptance remains open.
